11–14 MINConcept

What Makes a Drone a Drone?

1 · Flying machine

Motors spin propellers to create lift and controlled motion.

2 · Sensing system

Sensors measure the drone and its surroundings.

3 · Programmed agent

A controller or code sends ordered commands toward a goal.

Human comparison: sensors are the senses, the flight controller is the brain, and motors are the muscles.
?If a propeller spins but a sensor gives bad information, can the drone still complete its mission? Why?

52–56 MINStudent Challenge

Build a Safe Four-Block Mission

Foundation

Take off → hover 2 s → hover 1 s → land.
Goal: correct order and safe ending.

Core

Take off → forward 1 s at 20% → hover → land.
Goal: predict distance before running.

Stretch

Change one duration or power value. Record how the outcome changes.

Coder connects blocksSafety lead checks the zoneObserver records evidence
