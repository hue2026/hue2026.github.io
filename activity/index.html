Key Ideas for Today
  • Narrative arc gives a story its shape: beginning, rising action, climax, and resolution.
  • Flashback adds past information at the exact emotional moment the audience needs it.
  • Foreshadowing plants clues early so later events feel satisfying.
  • Cliffhanger ends a scene at peak tension so viewers need the next part.
  • Character development shows how a person changes through challenge and choice.
Think like a creator: do not only spot the technique—explain why a writer or director would choose it there.

Level 2 · Build a Storyboard Plan
What to do

Plan a 4-panel anime scene using at least two storytelling techniques. You may use your course character or invent a new one.

  • Give each panel a short caption.
  • Name the technique used in each panel.
  • End with a clear emotion you want the audience to feel.
Checklist
  • A clear beginning, middle, and ending.
  • At least 2 techniques correctly used.
  • Audience emotion named clearly.
  • Scene makes sense from panel to panel.

Level 3 · Design the Full Arc
What to build

Design a mini anime arc with the five stages of narrative arc. Include where you would place flashback, foreshadowing, cliffhanger, and character development.

  • Name the protagonist and central conflict.
  • Explain why each technique belongs where you placed it.
  • Show how the protagonist changes inside, not just outside.
